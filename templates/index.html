Project Overview

Electricity is a vital driver of economic activity, and its consumption reflects both industrial productivity and household energy access. The COVID-19 pandemic introduced a unique scenario for energy consumption analysis. With lockdowns enforced across India, various sectors saw drastic shifts in energy usage.

Key Scenarios Analyzed

From Jan 2019 to Dec 2020, India’s electricity consumption underwent changes influenced by seasonal demand, economic growth, and the COVID-19 lockdown.

Northern, Southern, Eastern, Western, and Northeastern states experienced differing usage levels due to climate, industrial presence, and population density.

Following the COVID-19 restrictions, energy needs rebounded in diverse ways. Some states exceeded pre-lockdown levels quickly, while others lagged.
